## Scalability Readiness Invariant

Invariant ID: SB15-performance-scale-proof

Before connected sandbox UI work, the simulation, visual mapping, WebGL bridge, command-batch, and snapshot paths must have executable scale evidence. The proof must record real timings and counts, not hand-entered performance rows.

## Required Scale Invariants

- At least 250 actors are normalized and materialized.
- At least 500 stores are normalized, materialized, hashed, and measured.
- At least 1000 scheduled events are materialized.
- At least 1000 visual actions are projected through the command-batch path.
- At least 500 staged WebGL commands are normalized.
- At least 100 snapshots are exported and imported with hash validation.
