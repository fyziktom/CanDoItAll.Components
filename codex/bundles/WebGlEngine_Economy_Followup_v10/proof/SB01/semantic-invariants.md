# Semantic invariants SB01

- The subbundle must not weaken generic/domain boundaries.
- The subbundle must not convert economic errors into visualization/runtime errors.
- The subbundle must not mark proof as complete without non-empty evidence artifacts.
- The current-state audit must map prepared weaknesses to owning closure subbundles before final closure.
- The audit must preserve cross-repo commit refs and proof-hygiene state as evidence, not as implementation proof.
