# Semantic invariants SB15

- The subbundle must not weaken generic/domain boundaries.
- The subbundle must not convert economic errors into visualization/runtime errors.
- The subbundle must not mark proof as complete without non-empty evidence artifacts.
- A closed proof manifest must fail validation when it cites a missing, empty, or blank bundle artifact.
- A screenshot can support proof only when paired with non-empty machine-readable assertions or diagnostics.
- A scan artifact that explicitly says the scan was skipped or not run cannot close proof.
