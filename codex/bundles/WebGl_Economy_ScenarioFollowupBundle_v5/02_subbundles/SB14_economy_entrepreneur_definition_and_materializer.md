# SB14 - Small entrepreneur scenario as definition and materializer

Repository: `CanDoItAll.Economy`

## Goal

Make the entrepreneur community scenario generic and loadable.

Actors:
- baker
- farmer
- carpenter
- customer-group
- local-fund

Locations:
- bakery
- farm
- workshop
- market
- fund-office

Events:
- production;
- sale;
- supply purchase;
- loan;
- delayed payment;
- repayment;
- investment.

Visual actions:
- buyer/seller flow;
- admin/writing pose for loan paperwork;
- movement to market/fund-office where useful;
- risk symbol for delayed payment.

No WebGL dependency.
