# Workflow alignment

Follow the CanDoItAll bundle workflow discipline:

- preserve raw inputs,
- keep requirements mapped to subbundles,
- execute subbundles in dependency order,
- keep proof artifacts under `proof/SBxx/`,
- record changed-file hashes,
- record command transcripts,
- keep closure honest and explicit,
- do not close unresolved gaps as prose-only residual risk.

This bundle is a follow-up hardening bundle, not a final demo implementation bundle.
